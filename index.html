03UX researchLow-fidelity coursework

Housing & Roommate Matching

A research-led low-fidelity concept that helps students evaluate housing options and roommate compatibility without losing the details that shape everyday living.

Challenge
Price, location, trust, and roommate compatibility are usually judged across disconnected tools.
Audience
Students looking for housing and compatible roommates.
Research
Focus group, affinity mapping, comparative evaluation, think-aloud testing, questionnaires, and semi-structured interviews.
Deliverable
Low-fidelity wireframes and an evaluated interaction direction—not a high-fidelity or implemented product.
Read full case study＋
01

Research

Understanding how students make housing decisions

A focus group identified four primary decision factors: affordability, location, trust, and roommate compatibility. A comparative evaluation of CollegePads and iROOMit then examined existing approaches to verification, filtering, and comparison.

Affordability + locationTrust + verificationHabits + dealbreakersClear comparisons
02

Define

Turning research themes into a clear user flow

The focus-group findings were organized through affinity mapping. The resulting themes defined a focused journey: set preferences, apply filters, review compatible matches, compare important living habits, and then save or connect.

Preferences→Filters→Matches→Compare→Connect
Low-fidelity only

The coursework produced wireframes for evaluating structure and flow—not a high-fidelity or coded product.

03

Evaluate

Evaluating the low-fidelity flow

Two participants completed think-aloud tasks using the low-fidelity wireframes, followed by a questionnaire and semi-structured interview. The evaluation supported the use of compatibility scores, verification, filters, and comparison. It also revealed unclear navigation, repeated questions, limited housing details, weak match explanations, and missing save confirmation.

Methods

  • Task observation
  • Questionnaire
  • Interview
Validated direction

Make comparison easy

Compatibility scores, verification, focused filters, and side-by-side details helped participants assess a match.

Usability problems

Reduce uncertainty

Navigation, housing details, recommendation logic, and save feedback needed another iteration.

Next design pass

Shorten and explain

Merge repeated inputs, add progress and back navigation, explain match scores, and show a persistent saved state.
